Your hardware (RTX 3050 6GB + i5-13420H) is solid for local AI via Ollama, but stay in the 3B–8B range at Q4_K_M (or similar) quantization. Larger models (13B+) usually force heavy CPU offload and become too slow. Expect roughly 15–40+ tokens/sec depending on the model, context length, and whether the GPU is fully loaded. Keep context modest (2k–4k tokens) for best speed and to avoid OOM.
